Work Day

Lesson Objectives

  • Document their thoughts and processes in their Engineering Notebooks
  • Test their designs utilizing the Forward, Lift, Reverse Activity

Lesson Overview

  • The purpose of this lesson is to provide students with additional time to build their arm, as well as give them the opportunity to test it.
  • In addition, students will document their design and build in their engineering notebooks.

Materials Needed

  • VEX IQ Robotics Kit components
  • Improve Your Arm Practice Activity
  • Engineering Notebooks

Getting Started

  • Begin with a whole group discussion about what students learned last lesson about arms and their functions. Ask students to share any potential helpful things they learned.

  • Discussion prompts:
    • What kind of arms did you consider?
    • What are some constraints or criteria for your arms?
      • Think: what does the arm need to be able to do?

Monitoring Progress

  • As students are working, circulate around the classroom and make sure that students are making progress on their designs.
  • Encourage students to test with the activity in between designs, and document their progress in their engineering notebooks.
  • If students are struggling, ask them some facilitation questions which can help to guide them along.

  • Facilitation Questions:
    • Are you satisfied with the current state of your arm?
      • Is there a specific way to test if your arm is good enough?
    • What are some potential improvements you can make to it?

Wrapping Up

  • As the class draws to a close, have students store their work in the storage area, and direct them to reflect on the day in their engineering notebooks, using the documentation prompts if needed.
  • Discussion Prompts:
    • How did you change or improve your arm today?
    • What are your plans for the next class?
    • How do you feel about your progress today?
    • What didn’t go as well today?
      • What could you do differently tomorrow to have more success?

Assessment

  • Successful progress of activity
  • Evidence of documenting

Preparation for next class

Next class will be a continuation of the independent work. Students will be provided with additional time to work on their arm designs and document them in their engineering notebooks.

Resources

  • Poster Guidelines
    • The Real World Solution poster should consist of, at least:
      • The problem being solved
      • One drawing of the imagined solution
      • Three briefly described details of the solution
      • The method by which the solution solves the problem
      • What type or types of engineering the solution falls under
      • Why the solution falls under that type (or types)
    • This is a minimum, so creativity is highly encouraged and students are free to go over this minimum as much as they like, as long as all the requirements are met
    • Most of all, the poster should be fun to both make and look at.
    • When presenting, all students involved in creating should speak for a fairly even amount of time with the others, but students can split up the subjects in whatever way they please.
