Disassembly and Looking Forward

Lesson Objectives

  • Completely deconstruct projects for storage
  • Reflect on learning and experiences
  • Brainstorm future applications of engineering skills

Lesson Overview

Students reflect and disassemble robots so they can be used for next year and provide feedback.

Materials Needed

  • VEX IQ Robotics Kit components
  • Paper and Pencil

Getting Started

  • Students carefully disassemble robots. (30 mins)
  • Begin discussion while disassembling:
    • Favorite parts of the curriculum?
    • Favorite activity?
    • Which challenge was most enjoyable?

Monitoring Progress

  • Move around classroom ensuring proper disassembly
  • Assist students with parts placement back in kits

Wrapping Up

  • Class discussion and written feedback using paper/pencil. (20 mins)
  • Discussion prompts:
    • Disliked anything?
    • What did you learn?
    • Applications outside classroom?
    • What would you have liked to learn more/less about?
    • If you could change anything, what would it be?

Assessment

  • Feedback form completed
  • Robots disassembled and stored correctly

Preparation for next class

Final class; ensure parts are organized and stored for future use.
