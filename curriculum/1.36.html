Disassembly and Looking Forward

Lesson Objectives

  • Completely deconstruct their projects for proper storage
  • Reflect on what they enjoyed, didn’t enjoy, learned, and still wish to learn
  • Brainstorm ways to keep applying their Engineering skills inside and outside the classroom

Materials Needed

  • VEX IQ Robotics Kit components
  • Paper and Pencil

Lesson Procedure

Overview

The purpose of this lesson is to wrap up the RISE Curriculum. Students will reflect and disassemble their robots so they can be used next year. Feedback will also be taken.

Getting Started

Students take out their robots and carefully disassemble them. (30 mins)

While disassembling, lead a discussion with these prompts:

  • What was your favorite part of these lessons? Why?
  • What was your favorite activity?
  • Which challenge did you like more, and why?

Monitoring Progress

  • Student discussion prompts reflection but is informal.
  • Ensure students are taking apart robots properly and putting all parts back into kits.
  • Assist students if needed and check that kits are intact.

Wrapping Up

Once disassembly is complete, hold a whole-class discussion. Students use paper and pencil to provide feedback. (20 mins)

  • Was there anything you disliked about the course?
  • What have you learned?
  • Where can you apply what you’ve learned?
  • What would you have liked to learn more about?
  • Was there anything you would have spent less time on?
  • If you could change any part of the lessons or course, what would you change?

Assessment

  • Students filled out feedback forms
  • Students took apart robots and stored parts properly without damage

Preparation for next class

This is the final class of the curriculum. Ensure all parts are organized and well stored for the future.
