Lesson 3: Importance of Pseudocode

Lesson Objectives

  • Learn what pseudocode is and why it is important in robotics.
  • Write and test simple pseudocode for a robot movement task.

Materials Needed

  • Notebooks for each student
  • Pseudocode document

Lesson Procedure

Overview

This lesson’s purpose is to introduce students to the idea of pseudocode and the importance of planning before writing code. Students will learn what pseudocode is, why it is used, and how it helps to organize thoughts and break down a problem into logical steps. They will practice writing pseudocode for a simple task that a robot could perform, and test it as a class.

Getting Started

  • Introduce the definition of pseudocode and discuss why programmers write pseudocode before coding.
  • Walk through the pseudocode document together to see the format and expectations.
  • Present a simple movement task (e.g., drive and turn) and ask students to outline steps in pseudocode as a class.

Monitoring Progress

  • Check that each step is clear, ordered, and testable as written.
  • Encourage students to revise steps to remove ambiguity.

Wrapping Up

  • Share out pseudocode examples and discuss improvements students would make before converting to blocks.

Assessment

  • Students understand what pseudocode is and why it should be done for every program
  • Students understand how to create an outline for a task and the format for a pseudocode

Preparation for next class

In the next class, we will go back to the computers and the robots, and the students will code and run the pentagon challenge today while learning about a couple of blocks and how they work.
