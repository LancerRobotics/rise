VEX IQ Kit Introduction

Lesson Objectives

  • Be able to identify and sort different robotics parts by category
  • Complete the Scavenger Hunt activity
  • Begin generating possible ideas for what they can do with the parts

Lesson Overview

Students will learn the different parts in their robotics kits as well as the different categories that they belong to. They will become familiar with the components, which will enable them to complete future activities faster. Finally, they will gain experience with taking out and putting away the components, making future lessons quicker.

Materials Needed

  • VEX IQ Robotics Kit components
  • Computer or Tablet
  • Interactive Parts Poster
  • Pencil and Paper
  • Vex IQ Activity - Scavenger Hunt

Getting Started

  • Open a kit and demonstrate to the students what it looks like. Open up the Interactive Parts Poster on your computer or tablet and show the students what each grouping of parts is while pointing to them in the VEX IQ Kit. Once each grouping is explained, open the floor for students to ask about specific parts and the whole class can learn about them throught the poster. (20 mins)
  • Separate students into pairs, triplets, or whatever structure evenly divides the class to have one group per kit. Distribute pencil and paper to each student, and then have them complete the IQ Activity - Scavenger Hunt, skipping the first step and instead identifying parts directly in their kits.

  • Discussion Prompts:
    • Why do we have to keep parts organized?
    • How would we have issues if one of the parts was lost?
    • How can we make sure no parts are lost when we use them?

Monitoring Progress

  • As Students complete the activity, have them try to write down the exact name of each part they find using the Interactive Parts Poster or the guide inside their Kits, as well as one potential use they can think of for the part (if applicable, a different use than the one listed on the scavenger hunt.) Use the questions below to prompt their thinking, if necessary. (20 mins)

  • Facilitation Questions:
    • How can you use two or more parts together?
    • Imagine a robot. How could you use the parts that you found to make it better?
    • How could you use the robot parts to help improve things in your everyday life?

Wrapping Up

  • Come back together for a whole group discussion about the activity. Ask how the activity went, and what was hard about it. Have every group pair up with another group and talk about their findings. Have the groups switch their papers and compare them. (10 mins)

  • Discussion Prompts:
    • If the robotics kits were mixed up, do you think you would be able to complete the activity again faster?
    • How important is having an organized robotics kit?

Assessment

  • Strong participation from everyone in activity (lays foundation for all other days)
  • Students handle and store the parts carefully and make sure to not lose anything

Preparation for next class

Next class will focus on basic geometric thinking and reasoning. Make sure that no students lost any parts and make sure all robotics kits are organized for the next day’s activity.
