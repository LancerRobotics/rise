Work Day for Engineering Poster

Lesson Objectives

  • Continue the Real World Solutions Poster
  • Learn about the ways students’ classroom skills apply to the real world as well as real careers

Lesson Overview

This lesson will be a work day for the Real-World Solution poster. Students will finish their posters and prepare to present them to the class in the next lesson.

Materials Needed

  • Students' Engineering Notebooks
  • Pencil and Paper
  • Poster Board, markers/colored pencils, or any other craft materials if available
  • Engineering Careers Reference Sheet

Getting Started

  • Begin with a brief class discussion on what problems students have chosen to solve, what kind of engineering they think they’re using, and any solutions they might already have. (5 mins)
  • After the discussion, put any posterboard, paper, markers, colored pencils, and any other craft materials available out so students can freely use them to create their posters, as well as copies of the Engineering Careers Reference Sheet if possible. Allow students to break into their groups and begin their work.

  • Discussion prompts:
    • What problem are you trying to solve?
    • Do you have a solution yet? If so, what is it?
    • What kind or kinds of engineering have you thought to use in your solution?

Monitoring Progress

  • As students work, monitor their progress and offer to answer any questions students may have. Offer help to any students who may need it. If timechecks are necessary for groups to better manage their time, offer those as well. Students should be able to complete their work mostly independently, though they may request to see the Engineering Careers Reference Sheet again to read themselves if individual copies weren’t available. (40 mins)

Wrapping Up

  • At the end of class, collect students’ posters and store them somewhere safe for the next lesson. Clean up all craft materials and put them away. (5 mins)

Assessment

  • Active collaboration among groupmates, if applicable
  • Successful completion of the Real World Solutions Poster

Preparation for next class

Next class will involve students presenting their projects, so it may be best to review the posters outside of class before the next lesson to ensure they meet all the guidelines. Additionally, there will be some light hardware work with the kits, but the robot batteries will not be needed.

Resources

  • Poster Guidelines
    • The Real World Solution poster should consist of, at least:
      • The problem being solved
      • One drawing of the imagined solution
      • Three briefly described details of the solution
      • The method by which the solution solves the problem
      • What type or types of engineering the solution falls under
      • Why the solution falls under that type (or types)
    • This is a minimum, so creativity is highly encouraged and students are free to go over this minimum as much as they like, as long as all the requirements are met
    • Most of all, the poster should be fun to both make and look at.
    • When presenting, all students involved in creating should speak for a fairly even amount of time with the others, but students can split up the subjects in whatever way they please.
