Work Day

Lesson Objectives

  • Document their thoughts and processes in their Engineering Notebooks
  • Test their designs utilizing the Improve your Claw practice activity
  • Optimize their manipulators through testing and iteration

Lesson Overview

Today will be a work day, focused on improving the robot claw designs. This unit is a bit challenging, so students will be given multiple work days to work on building and improving their robot. Emphasize intent in this lesson, encouraging students to make specific choices rather than wildly experimenting to see what works.

Materials Needed

  • Engineering Notebooks
  • VEX IQ Kits
  • Improve your Claw activity
  • Timer

Getting Started

  • Begin with a class discussion about their progress so far. Ask students to share their designs so far, what they’ve learned, and why they’re choosing the designs they are. (5 mins)
  • Allow the students to break into their Bot Groups, retrieve their materials, and begin working. While they do, set up the field according to the Improve your Claw activity. Remind students that while they are working today, they should be documenting all aspects of their progress within their engineering notebooks. (40 mins)

  • Discussion prompts:
    • What have you learned so far?
    • How has your documentation helped you?
    • What design choices are you making?
      • Why are you making them?
    • Have you found anything that works particularly well? Not well?

Monitoring Progress

  • As students are working, make sure to walk around and ask them about the improvements that they are making to their claws. Ask them about the feasibility of their changes, and how their designs would help them accomplish their objective. Ask them what the purpose of the designs are. Look for signs of improvement and iteration. Encourage students to document all the time with the prompts provided.

  • Documentation Prompts:
    • How are your changes going to help you accomplish your objective faster?
    • What are you trying to accomplish with your modifications?
      • Make the robot faster? Easier to pick things up?
    • What have you changed from the initial claw design?
      • What have you kept the same?
    • What is your strategy in completing the challenge?

Wrapping Up

  • Come back together for a whole group discussion about their designs. Ask them what kind of modifications they made, and if they are willing to share them with the rest of the group. (5 mins)
  • Discussion Prompts:
    • What changes or improvements have you made today?
    • What strategies are you using to document your progress?
    • What are your ideas for the next class?
    • What’s one thing that went well in your group today?
      • What was something that challenged you?

Assessment

  • Respectful and lively discussion and collaboration
  • Documentation of every step in Engineering Notebooks
    • Adjustments made
    • Observations recorded
    • peer/team contributions

Preparation for next class

Next class will be another work day for the clawbot. This section of the curriculum will consist mostly of work days, so make sure that all devices are charged and plugged in and that all clawbots are safely stored so students can work on them the next day.

Resources

  • Poster Guidelines
    • The Real World Solution poster should consist of, at least:
      • The problem being solved
      • One drawing of the imagined solution
      • Three briefly described details of the solution
      • The method by which the solution solves the problem
      • What type or types of engineering the solution falls under
      • Why the solution falls under that type (or types)
    • This is a minimum, so creativity is highly encouraged and students are free to go over this minimum as much as they like, as long as all the requirements are met
    • Most of all, the poster should be fun to both make and look at.
    • When presenting, all students involved in creating should speak for a fairly even amount of time with the others, but students can split up the subjects in whatever way they please.
