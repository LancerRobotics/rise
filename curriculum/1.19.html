Disassembling the Claw, Poster Presentation Day

Lesson Objectives

  • Partially disassemble their robots to prepare for the next weeks of lessons
  • Present their Real World Solution posters
  • Understand other students’ projects and the different types of engineering.

Lesson Overview

The purpose of this lesson is for students to share their project posters with the rest of their class, as well as learn about the different projects their classmates completed. The secondary purpose is to have students disassemble the intakes on their robots so the materials can be reused for future lessons.

Materials Needed

  • VEX IQ Robotics Kit components
  • Students’ Robots
  • Created Real World Solutions Poster

Getting Started

  • Begin by allowing students to split into their project groups and decide about when in the order they want to present. Before any students begin presenting, remind the class to be good audience members and pay attention, because they would want the same respect while they present. Inform them that after each group presents they can take some time to answer questions, so they should save them until the end, and each group will have about 3 minutes to present and answer questions after. Allow the first group to go up in front of the class and begin the series of presentations. (35 mins)

Monitoring Progress

  • After all the presentations are finished, collect the students’ posters and give one last round of applause for their work. Have students gather into their Bot Groups, and retrieve their kits and robots.
  • Once all bot groups have all their materials, instruct students to carefully disassemble their intake mechanisms that they had created for the Robot Soccer Challenge. Ensure that students understand that they are ONLY disassembling the intake, and should leave the BaseBot intact as a foundation to start the next unit with. (10-15 mins)

Wrapping Up

  • As students finish disassembling, have them return their kits to the storage area, taking care that they’re put away properly and have all of the parts that go with them.

Assessment

  • Active participation in the presentation of created posters
  • Properly deconstructed robot, prepared for next lesson

Preparation for next class

The next lesson will begin the final project of the curriculum. To be prepared for this, it is crucial that all kits are fully intact with all the original materials. If possible, double-check each of the kits to make sure that they’re ready for the next lesson and unit.
