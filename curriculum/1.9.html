Building and Documenting Conjoined

Lesson Objectives

  • Finish their robots by building and documenting their creation of the claw attachment
  • Gain an understanding of the ways hardware and software interact through specific mechanisms, such as a claw

Lesson Overview

Today is for finishing the construction portion of the Robot Soccer Challenge in order to make way for the design portion next week. This day conjoins multiple covered topic in the week, and as a result will most likely challenge students, but to a reasonable degree - there are tutorials readily available in case any student comes up against an obstacle they just can’t get over themselves.

Materials Needed

  • VEX IQ Kits and Basebots
  • Students' Engineering Notebooks
  • Simple Clawbot Instructions

Getting Started

Begin with a whole group discussion about how students will be combining their skills today to finish getting ready for the challenge. Tell them now is the time for questions, before they go off to work. When finished, have them retrieve their BaseBots with their Bot Groups. (5 mins)


  • Discussion Prompts:
    • What do you think are some challenges of building and documenting at the same time?
    • How do you think you should go about putting these skills together?

    • Monitoring Progress

    • When students have their BaseBots, distribute the VEX kits and let them begin constructing their Claw as according to the Simple Clawbot Instructions (25-30 mins)
    • When they have finished, direct students to try making a multiview drawing of their new Clawbot, and encourage them to take their time and pay close attention to detail. (10-15 mins)

    • Additional Notes:
      • Be ready to answer questions, very likely the same one over and over
      • Make sure students’ nametag pieces are still clearly visible after attaching the claw, and that they’ve relocated the piece if necessary
      • Remind students to document their work in their Engineering Notebooks not just with the drawing but also their building process

    Wrapping Up

    • When students have finished building and drawing, have them write some about their experience using the documentation prompts provided, either below their multiview drawing or on a new page. (5 mins)

    • Discussion Prompts:
      • What were some things that went well today?
      • What were some challenges you faced?
      • What is something you’d do or would have done differently if you could?

    Assessment

    • Successful completion of Simple Clawbot
    • Day is documented properly in Engineering Notebook

    Preparation for next class

    Next class will begin the learning required for the first real design challenge of the course, so it’s important that students finish their work today. They should be able to finish all the work in time, but if not, any missed work can be completed in the upcoming work days.
