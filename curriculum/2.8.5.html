Lesson 8.5: Nested Loops

Lesson Objectives

  • Review loops and learn how nested loops work in VEXcode
  • Use nested loops to create a program that helps their robot traverse a path

Materials Needed

  • Computers (1–2 per group)
  • Basebots
  • VEXcode IQ website
  • Notebooks and writing utensils for each student

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

  • In this lesson, students will review loops and use their knowledge to complete a task using nested loops. They will get experience in how to use different types of loops in their program to execute the robot functions.

2. Getting Started

  • Refresh students’ memories on loops
  • Review the notes taken from last class
  • Complete a quick task: Drive the robot backwards 5 times for 100 mm each

3. Monitoring Progress

  • Allow the students to go through a series of tasks to increase their familiarity with nested loops
  • Have the students use their computers and set up a drivebase like they did in previous lessons. Make sure that the ports selected are correct!
  • Task: Nested Loops
    • Think back to how they created one continuous square with the forever loop

4. Wrapping Up

  • Now, students should discuss in groups and with other groups on how they found a solution to complete the task.
  • Have a short class discussion about what they found different, interesting, or more difficult/easier about nested loops.

Assessment

  • Students understand what the different types of loops are, how they are used, why they are used, and the benefits they offer.
  • Students understand how to use nested loops to make their programs more efficient.

Preparation for next class

In the next class, the students will come to class with their knowledge about how to use loops and think about how to complete the nested loops task if their group was unable to complete it.
