Lesson 14: Follow the Tape Challenge

Objective(s)

  • Understand the rules of the new challenge
  • Brainstorm some ideas on how to solve the problem

Materials Needed

  • Notebooks and writing utensils for each student
  • Path layout

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

  • In this lesson, we will introduce a new challenge that the students will be working on for the next couple of days.
  • Students will brainstorm and discuss some ideas they have.
  • The rest of the class will be for writing pseudocode for the project.

2. Getting Started

  • Begin by explaining how the challenge will work:
    • There will be a path made of tape on the ground, made up of different line segments.
    • Students will have to write code to make their robots drive autonomously on the line.
    • The group that finishes the fastest wins.

3. Monitoring Progress

  • Give the students a couple minutes to discuss among their group on how to do the challenge.
  • Then come back as a class and discuss:
    • How will you complete the path in the shortest possible time?
    • What are some challenges you might face?
    • How will things change if you didn’t know the layout beforehand?

4. Wrapping Up

  • Students should spend the rest of the period working with their groups on their pseudocode for the project.

Assessment

  • Students should understand the rules of the challenge.
  • Students should have a clear idea of what their plan is going forward.

Preparation for next class

The next class will be entirely used to work on and test their code for this project, so teachers should do their best to lay out the path on the ground using tape. Teachers can also start doing this while the students are working on their pseudocode.
