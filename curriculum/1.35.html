Lesson 35: Debate Day 2

Objective(s):

  • Conduct a series of respectful debates
  • Demonstrate knowledge of assigned robotics topics
  • Learn about other types of robotics through classmates’ arguments

Materials Needed:

  • Students’ Engineering Notebooks
  • Projector/Whiteboard/Chalkboard

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

Students continue demonstrating their knowledge through multiple debates. By hearing other groups present, they gain exposure to robotics topics they did not research personally.

2. Getting Started (5 mins)

Display the debate matchups so all students can see. Allow groups to gather, review their arguments, and prepare mentally. Once ready, explain the debate procedure and begin Round 2 debates.

Debate Pairings – Round 2

  • Autonomous Drones vs Space Robotics
  • Humanoid Robots vs Robot Dogs
  • Medical Robotics vs Manufacturing Robotics
  • Agricultural Robotics vs Aquatic Robots

Debate Structure

Each debate lasts 10 minutes with a 5-minute buffer. The structure is identical to Day 1:

Stage 1: Opening Statements (2 min each)

Groups present their full argument without interruption.

Stage 2: Rebuttal Preparation (2 min)

Groups simultaneously prepare a rebuttal based on their opponent’s opening arguments.

Stage 3: Rebuttals (2 min each)

Groups deliver their rebuttals or provide additional information not covered earlier.

After each debate, students record the winner for that round in their Engineering Notebooks under “Round 2.”

3. Monitoring Progress (45 mins)

  • Ensure the next debate groups are ready to begin immediately
  • Encourage all students to take notes during every debate
  • Debaters should take careful notes during the opening statements for use during rebuttals
  • All students should have an opportunity to speak across the stages
  • Maintain respectful behavior and silence during the speaking turns of others

4. Wrapping Up

If time allows, students choose who they believe is the overall “winner” of the robotics debates across all categories.

Assessment

  • Clear, confident, and respectful speaking
  • Proper debate conduct and sportsmanship
  • Thoughtful notes and winner selections recorded in Engineering Notebooks
  • Engagement in both opening statements and rebuttals

Preparation for Next Class

The next lesson is the final session of the course. Students will not need Engineering Notebooks for that class, although teachers may choose to collect them at the end of this lesson. However, we recommend allowing students to keep their notebooks as a personal record of their learning, engineering notes, and project documentation. The next lesson will also be the final time they work with the robotics kits.
