Debate Day 2

Lesson Objectives

  • Conduct respectful debates
  • Demonstrate knowledge of assigned robotics topics
  • Learn about other types of robotics through classmates’ arguments

Lesson Overview

Students continue debates from the previous lesson with new pairings to demonstrate knowledge and learn about other robotics types.

Materials Needed

  • Students’ Engineering Notebooks
  • Projector/Whiteboard/Chalkboard

Getting Started

  • Display debate matchups so students can see and go to their groups. (5 mins)
  • Debate Pairings - Round 2:
    • Autonomous Drones vs Space Robotics
    • Humanoid Robots vs Robot Dogs
    • Medical Robotics vs Manufacturing Robotics
    • Agricultural Robotics vs Aquatic Robots
  • Debate Structure:
    • 10 minutes per debate, 5 min buffer
    • 3 stages: Opening Statements (2 min each), Rebuttal Preparation (2 min), Rebuttals (2 min each)

Monitoring Progress

  • Conduct debates in the given order. (45 mins)
  • Encourage note-taking and active participation
  • Ensure each student has a chance to speak at least once

Wrapping Up

  • Students decide on the overall winner if time allows and write in Engineering Notebooks

Assessment

  • Clear and confident speaking
  • Proper conduct and respectful behavior
  • Notes on debates and decisions on winners

Preparation for next class

Next lesson will be the final of the course; students won’t need notebooks, but can keep them for reference.
