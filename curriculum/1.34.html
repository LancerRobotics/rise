Lesson 34: Debate Day 1

Objective(s):

  • Conduct a series of respectful debates
  • Demonstrate knowledge of assigned robotics topics
  • Learn about other robotics areas through peer arguments

Materials Needed:

  • Students’ Engineering Notebooks
  • Projector, Whiteboard, or Chalkboard

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

Students will demonstrate their knowledge from the previous lesson through structured debates. These debates also expose students to robotics topics they did not personally research.

2. Getting Started (5 mins)

Display the debate matchups where everyone can see them. Allow students to gather in their groups and review their arguments. Once groups are ready, explain the debate structure and begin the first round.

Debate Pairings – Round 1

  • Humanoid Robots vs Aquatic Robots
  • Manufacturing Robotics vs Agricultural Robotics
  • Robot Dogs vs Autonomous Drones
  • Space Robotics vs Medical Robotics

Debate Structure

Each debate lasts 10 minutes with a 5-minute buffer. There are 3 stages:

Stage 1: Opening Statements (2 min each)

Each group presents their full argument with no interruptions.

Stage 2: Rebuttal Preparation (2 min)

Groups simultaneously prepare a rebuttal using notes from the opening statements.

Stage 3: Rebuttals (2 min each)

Groups deliver their rebuttal or additional supporting information.

After each debate, students write in their Engineering Notebooks which group they believe won for that round.

3. Monitoring Progress (45 mins)

  • Ensure the next groups are ready immediately to keep debates moving
  • Encourage students to take notes during all debates
  • Debaters must take notes on the opposing team to prepare rebuttals
  • Encourage shy students to participate at least once
  • Maintain respectful conduct and silence during speaking turns

4. Wrapping Up

If time allows, students decide who they believe is the overall winner among all robotics categories debated.

Assessment

  • Clear, confident speaking
  • Respectful debate behavior
  • Complete notes and winner selections in Engineering Notebooks
  • Thoughtful participation in rebuttals

Preparation for Next Class

The next lesson follows the same debate structure but uses new group matchups.
