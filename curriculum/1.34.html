Debate Day 1

Lesson Objectives

  • Conduct a series of respectful debates
  • Demonstrate knowledge of their assigned robotics topic
  • Learn about other types of robotics through other students’ arguments

Materials Needed

  • Students’ Engineering Notebooks
  • Projector/Whiteboard/Chalkboard

Lesson Procedure

Overview

Students will demonstrate knowledge acquired in the last lesson with multiple small debates. These debates also educate students on topics they didn’t research by listening to classmates’ arguments.

Getting Started

Display debate matchups, allow students to go to their groups, and review their arguments. (5 mins)

Debate Pairings - Round 1:
  • Humanoid Robots vs Aquatic Robots
  • Manufacturing Robotics vs Agricultural Robotics
  • Robot Dogs vs Autonomous Drones
  • Space Robotics vs Medical Robotics
Debate Structure:
  • Debates are 10 minutes long with 5 minutes buffer.
  • Three stages:
    • Opening statements: 2 minutes per group
    • Rebuttal Preparation: 2 minutes per group (simultaneous)
    • Rebuttals: 2 minutes per group
  • Students record which group they think won in their notebooks under round headings.

Monitoring Progress

Conduct debates in order. (45 mins)

Tips to run smoothly:
  • Next groups ready to debate immediately after the previous group.
  • All students take notes on each group’s points.
  • Debating students should take notes on opponents’ opening statements and rebuttals.
  • Encourage shy students to speak at least once.

Wrapping Up

Students try to decide on the overall winner of the round and write it in their Engineering Notebooks.

Assessment

  • Clear and confident speaking in debates
  • Proper conduct during debates
  • Notes on debates and personal decision on winners

Preparation for next class

The next lesson repeats this one with different debate groups; the procedure is the same.
