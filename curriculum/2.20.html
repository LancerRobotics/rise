Lesson 20: Test Day

Objective(s)

  • Students will be tested on their knowledge of the VEX IQ curriculum in a friendly competition of the follow the tape game.

Materials Needed

  • Notebooks
  • Computers
  • Basebots
  • VEXcode IQ

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

  • In this lesson, students will compete to see which group can follow the tape the best.

2. Getting Started

  • Lay down a randomly generated tape path on the ground, where each group will go up, and based on the amount it takes to follow all of the tape, and how well the bot managed to follow the tape, will decide who the winner is.
  • The teacher may incorporate a variety of factors that decides who the winner is, however the two mentioned above serve as a good criteria.

3. Monitoring Progress

  • Students will use the bots that they had, along with their extensive knowledge in the VEX IQ curriculum, and try their best in a friendly competition.
  • All groups should then write in their notebooks reflections on the following questions:
    • What could we have done to improve our bot?
    • What did we do well?
    • What challenges did we face making the bot, and how did we overcome them?

4. Wrapping Up

  • Students should spend the remaining time of class reflecting on other potential questions that they could ask themselves within their groups.

Assessment

  • Students should be able to spot weaknesses and strengths in their bot, and think of solutions that they could have implemented.
