Lesson 29: Reflection

Objective(s)

  • Students will reflect on their experience in the code curriculum.

Materials Needed

  • Notebooks
  • Computers
  • Basebots
  • VEXcode IQ

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

  • This final lesson is an opportunity for students to reflect on how much they learned and provide feedback on the curriculum.

2. Getting Started

  • Have each student share with their group about their favorite lesson or project and why.
  • Have each student share their least favorite lesson or project and why.
  • Have each student share their favorite block in VEXcode IQ.

3. Monitoring Progress

  • Hold a whole-class discussion and have each group present what they discussed.
  • As a class, talk about what students want to learn more about in robotics.
  • Ask whether students want to continue with code or try more build projects.

4. Wrapping Up

  • Give each student a notecard and have them anonymously write anything they want to share about the curriculum.
  • Encourage constructive criticism and collect the notecards at the end of class.

Assessment

  • Students reflected on the code curriculum and provided feedback on their learning experience.
