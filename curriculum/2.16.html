Lesson 16: Test Day

Lesson Objectives

  • Complete the code/finishing touches
  • Have each group go through the maze and carry out the challenge

Materials Needed

  • Auton plan from last class
  • Computers (ideally 1 per group)
  • Basebots
  • VEXcode IQ website

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

  • Review time to ensure the code is finished- finishing touches
  • Review the challenge: Each group must have their Basebot navigate through the tape maze successfully at least once.
    • Each group will do the challenge, time it for each group, group with lowest time wins
  • Review the challenge

2. Getting Started

  • Each group finalizes and uploads their code to their Basebot
  • Have a few short practice runs in order to verify:
    • The robot follows the tape maze correctly
    • Turning angles are correct
    • The Basebot does not cross the tape boundaries.

3. Monitoring Progress

  • Begin the official timed trials:
    • Pick an order for groups (Ex: spinning a wheel- randomize it)
    • Record each group's time to complete the maze
      • Each group should get two chances to successfully make it through the maze (depending on how much time there is in class)
      • If a Basebot crosses the line, they get one more chance and their new time for the second run is used.
  • Encourage students to troubleshoot between runs- not what worked and what didn’t/needs improvement.

4. Wrapping Up

  • Announce the winners of the competition (the group with the lowest completion time)
  • Reflection:
    • What strategies did your team implement that helped you succeed?
    • What was the hardest part of coding the autonomous path?
    • If you had more time, what would you improve?

Assessment

  • During the competition, observe how each group worked together and communicated.
  • Did each team…
    • Demonstrate a functioning autonomous program?
    • Complete the maze successfully OR make improvements as they went along?
  • Ask students to take note of what they learned today and think of ways they would approach the next challenge differently/improve their strategy.

Preparation for next class

Next class will begin an extra challenge where students will not know the path layout until the day of testing.
