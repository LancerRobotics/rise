Lesson 13: Exploration Day

Objective(s)

  • Have students explore the website and experiment with blocks they haven’t tried yet.

Materials Needed

  • Notebooks and writing utensils for each student
  • Computers (ideally 1 per group)
  • Basebots
  • VEXcode IQ website

Lesson Procedure

1. Overview

  • In this lesson, students will work with their groups to independently go through the website and work with some blocks of their choice that they haven’t tried before.

2. Getting Started

  • Begin by explaining how today’s lesson will be independent exploration, and tell the students to figure out how new blocks work. Some things they can try to do are:
    • Making the brain play a sound
    • Printing text on the brain
    • Drawing a shape on the brain

3. Monitoring Progress

  • Have students work with their groups on trying different things. Make sure they are on task, but try not to intervene too much if they are stuck or if they make a mistake. Trial and error is an important part of programming.

4. Wrapping Up

  • Come back together as a class and have each group share something new that they found out, along with how the block is used.

Assessment

  • Students learned something new about the website.
  • Students understand how to try different things to figure out how something works, rather than it just being told by the teacher.

Preparation for next class

The next class will introduce the challenge for the next few days.
