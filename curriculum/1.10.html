Manipulators and Intakes

Lesson Objectives

  • Learn the definition of a Manipulator and the definition of an Intake in robotics
  • Understand the difference and relationship between a manipulator and an intake system
  • Apply understanding through discussion, observation, and notebook reflection

Lesson Overview

This lesson is essentially just informational preparation for the next lesson, and so is fairly straightforward without a lot of teacher interaction and instruction. Nevertheless, resources are provided to facilitate students’ learning.

Materials Needed

  • Students' Engineering Notebooks
  • Computers/projector
  • Robot Soccer Lesson 2 (Learn Section)

Getting Started

  • Begin by having students (in groups) open up Robot Soccer Lesson 2 on their computers. Instruct them to watch the manipulator and intake videos in the learn section, and clarify that they can ignore the controller and motor group videos. Alternatively, set up a projector so the class can watch the videos together. (15 mins each)

  • Definitions Reference:
    • The definitions and details of the terms Manipulator and Intake are detailed in the Resources section in the Manipulator Definition Document and the Intake Definition Document.

Monitoring Progress

  • As students watch each video, they should take notes on one function of the system as well as one question they have, which they will then discuss with a partner or the class.
  • After each video, have students partner with each other to discuss what they took notes on and then the class should come together to answer the questions the students still have. (5-10 mins each)

  • Facilitation
    • The general structure of this lesson is alternating from video to discussion, which is why the timestamps are slightly different
    • To prompt students to discuss with their partner, have them each share what they wrote down, and then try to identify any more important parts of the video together.

Wrapping Up

  • After the class finishes both videos, have them take out their Engineering Notebooks and answer the review questions at the end of the learn section in them. (10 mins)
  • Finish the lesson by offering to answer any lingering questions that students have after completing the lesson. Prompt them to think about which intake system/manipulator they would want to use in their next designs. If desired, collect student’s engineering notebooks for evaluation and assessment.

Assessment

  • Properly answered summary questions in students’ Engineering Notebooks

Preparation for next class

Next class begins the design challenge proper, so being familiar with the material of today’s lesson videos will be helpful (so students can ask their instructor directly instead of turning to the computer). Make sure to give the Engineering Notebooks back to students at the beginning of next class if they were taken at the end of this one, because students will need them.
