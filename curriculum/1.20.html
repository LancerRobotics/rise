Starting a Bigger Challenge

Lesson Objectives

  • Learn the rules to the Up and Over Challenge
  • Construct the full Clawbot as according to building instructions

Lesson Overview

The purpose of this lesson is similar to the purpose lesson 6. Students will build a robot using guided instructions and learn the rules of the Up and Over Challenge, which they’ll be working on over the coming weeks.

Materials Needed

  • VEX IQ Robotics Kits
  • BaseBots
  • Up and Over Lesson 1: Introduction
  • ClawBot Build Instructions

Getting Started

  • Have students retrieve the materials they’ve been using for the Robot Soccer unit.
    • However, they should not assemble into their previous Bot Groups.
  • Divide the class into new groups, and reassign kits to them. Once groups are formed, have each new group write their names on a 2x8 “smooth” piece of their BaseBot, erasing any previous names. These new groups will become their new Bot Groups for the rest of the unit. (5 min)
  • Group Ideas:
    • Try grouping students with different observed skills with each other, to see if they can collaborate and learn from each other’s differences.
    • If your class is ready, they might be able to form their own groups, but make sure to stay watchful.
    • All in all, we simply highly recommend that the same groups from the Robot Soccer Unit aren’t used.

Monitoring Progress

  • Once the new Bot Groups are assigned, have the students begin following the ClawBot Build Instructions (35 mins)
  • Once all groups are done (or in a good stopping place and almost done), have the whole class watch the video of the Up and Over Challenge in Up and Over Lesson 1: Introduction. Explain the rules and answer any questions that the students have. (5-10 mins)

Wrapping Up

  • After reviewing the Challenge and watching the video, give groups time to complete their Clawbot if they haven’t already and there’s time left.
  • Once finished, all groups should clean up and save their work in the saving area as done in previous lessons. (5 mins)
  • Facilitation
    • The students should be able to finish within the time given, but if for some reason they can’t, just pause the class to watch the video and they can come back to their construction in the next lesson.
    • A good stopping place to pause students is where they can put their work in the saving area with as little loose pieces as possible.

Assessment

  • Successful completion of Full ClawBot

Preparation for next class

Next lesson begins a series of single lesson activities that allow the students to have fun with their robots. If there are any delays in work in this lesson, the students will have to complete their robot before they can participate in those activities. If the whole class needs more time, the curriculum can be adjusted to simply push each lesson one day back and remove one of the later work days to compensate.
