Work Day for Engineering Poster

Lesson Objectives

  • Continue the Real World Solutions Poster
  • Learn how classroom skills apply to the real world and careers

Lesson Overview

Students will continue working on their Real World Solutions posters and prepare them for presentation in the next lesson.

Materials Needed

  • Students' Engineering Notebooks
  • Pencil and Paper
  • Poster Board, markers/colored pencils, or any other craft materials
  • Engineering Careers Reference Sheet

Getting Started

  • Start with a discussion about problems students are solving, solutions, and types of engineering. (5 mins)
  • Set up materials and allow groups to start work.
  • Discussion prompts:
    • What problem are you trying to solve?
    • Do you have a solution yet?
    • What type of engineering applies to your solution?

Monitoring Progress

  • Observe groups, answer questions, provide time checks if necessary (40 mins)
  • Students can consult the Engineering Careers Reference Sheet as needed

Wrapping Up

  • Collect posters and materials (5 mins)

Assessment

  • Active collaboration
  • Successful completion of poster

Preparation for next class

Next lesson: Students will present their projects. Review posters before the class. Some light hardware work may be needed, batteries not required.
