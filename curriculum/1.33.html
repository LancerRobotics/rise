Making More Real World Connections

Lesson Objectives

  • Research robotics in the real world
  • Prepare to debate classmates respectfully

Materials Needed

  • Computers if available
  • Engineering Notebooks

Lesson Procedure

Overview

Students will research real-world robotics applications and prepare arguments for class debates.

Getting Started

Begin with a discussion: "What type of robotics do you think is the most important?" (5 mins)

Discussion Prompts:
  • How can robotics be used in real life?
  • What different kinds of robots can you think of?
  • Which do you think is the best or most important?
  • Which elements of robotics and the EDP can you see in daily life?

Monitoring Progress

Assign groups to research robotics categories: Robot Dogs, Humanoid Robots, Medical Robots, Space Robots, Autonomous Drones, Robotic Manufacturing, Agricultural Robots, Aquatic Robots. (40 mins)

Research Prompts:
  • Why is this type of robotics important?
  • What can it do that others can’t?
  • Why is this part important?
  • How does this support your argument?

Wrapping Up

  • Share findings and collect notebooks (5 mins)

Assessment

  • Active discussion and collaboration
  • Detailed research notes in Engineering Notebooks

Preparation for next class

Students will debate their assigned robots; notebooks must be ready.
