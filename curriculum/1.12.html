Developing Designs

Lesson Objectives

  • Continue work from the last lesson
  • Utilize the Manipulators in Motion activity to gain feedback from testing and pursue improvements to their designs
  • Apply problem-solving and engineering learnings for iterations on previous ideas

Lesson Overview

This work day is meant to continue the process begun in that last lesson, so students can work on their manipulator designs using the practice activity provided.

Materials Needed

  • Students' Engineering Notebooks
  • VEX IQ Field Materials
  • VEX IQ kits and Clawbots
  • Manipulators in Motion Activity

Getting Started

  • Begin by having students break off into their Bot Groups and retrieve all the necessary materials. While they retrieve their materials, set up a field matching the Manipulators in Motion Activity. Once students and the field are ready, they can begin to work. (5 mins)
  • As the students begin to work, remind them to iterate and experiment-come up with lots of ideas, and use those ideas to make new ideas. If they aren’t sure if something will work, test it and record what happens.

Monitoring Progress

  • As students are working, circulate around the classroom and look and listen for student engagement. Encourage students to “think out loud”, and ask them their ideas and thought processes. Answer any questions students might have, and offer help, but try to let them sort out problems on their own. (45 mins)
  • Encourage students to use design sketches, adjustments, and notes to keep track of the iterations, and to follow the Manipulators in Motion Activity if they’re not sure where to go next.
  • If a successful iteration is made, prompt students to evaluate what went well and what didn’t, think about their next steps (action plan), and write that all down in their engineering notebooks

  • Documentation Prompts:
    • What have I done that has worked? What hasn’t worked?
      • Why do I think that is?
    • What should I do next?
    • What step of the EDP am I doing right now?

Wrapping Up

  • Have students save their work in the saving area designated for their Robots, and clean up any materials that aren’t part of an active construction. Have students briefly reflect on what they would like to accomplish tomorrow and what went well today in their Engineering Notebooks. (5 mins)

  • Reflection Prompts:
    • What's one challenge I encountered?
    • What is one specific task I want to work on next time?
    • What is one improvement my group made to our robot?

    • Assessment

    • Documentation of every step in the Engineering Notebook
    • Lively conversation as students complete iterations
    • Evidence of hands-on iterations and tests

    Preparation for next class

    The next class will be more like a regular lesson, since it introduces the second round of testing and students will require some teaching before they can return to independent work. Like last lesson, saving the field is helpful, but not strictly necessary.
