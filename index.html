About us

RISE is a student-led initiative bringing robotics education to young learners at the GRACE Academic Center in Kisii, Kenya, in collaboration with the nonprofit organization TEEEM. We've raised about $6,000 to provide VEX-IQ kits sufficient for 50 students, with 30 students currently actively participating in the program.

Support Us

Help us bring robotics education to Kenya! Your support enables us to provide VEX-IQ kits and create a sustainable STEM curriculum for students at the GRACE Academic Center.


With your help, we can provide students with hands-on STEM learning through robotics. 100% of funds raised will go toward purchasing a VEX IQ Classroom Bundle, equipping students with everything they need to build, program, and compete with their own robots.
