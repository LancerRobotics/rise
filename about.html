About us

We are working on a project to bring robotics education to the students of the GRACE Academic Center in Kisii, Kenya, in collaboration with the nonprofit organization TEEEM. Our goal is to raise $6,000 to purchase VEX-IQ kits and create a sustainable robotics curriculum. This program will teach elementary and middle schoolers fundamental building and coding concepts, empowering them with skills for the future. The GRACE Academic Center is a co-ed day school with around 120 students, known for its commitment to high-quality education and support for disadvantaged children. The school, a registered KPSA member with fully certified teachers, provides nutritious meals and basic health care, boasting a 100% passing rate on the high school entrance exam. Our collaboration with TEEEM and the dedicated teachers at GRACE will ensure that this robotics curriculum can be used for years to come, fostering a love for STEM in young learners.
